# AZ-900 Azure Fundamentals – Focused Study Plan

This repository documents a **time-efficient, exam-focused study plan** for the  
**AZ-900: Microsoft Azure Fundamentals** certification.

It combines:
- ✅ **John Savill’s AZ-900 video series** (best signal-to-noise)
- ✅ **Official Microsoft instructor-led AZ-900 videos** (official wording & framing)

Target audience:
- Engineers, developers, analysts, architects
- Strong technical background
- Goal: **pass confidently**, not over-study

---

## 🧠 Study Strategy

- **Primary source:** John Savill (exam-focused explanations)
- **Secondary source:** Microsoft official videos (terminology alignment)
- Focus on:
  - Definitions
  - Use cases
  - Comparisons
- Avoid:
  - Deep configuration detail
  - SKU-level or pricing minutiae

Time commitment:
- Weekdays: **60–75 mins**
- Weekends: **~90 mins**

## 🧪 Exam Technique Tips

- [ ] Read every word of each question
- [ ] Look for:
  - “Best”
  - “Least administrative effort”
  - “Most cost-effective”
- [ ] When unsure, choose the **simplest, most managed** service
